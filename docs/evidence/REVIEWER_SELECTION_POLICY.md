# Reviewer Selection Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines a simple reviewer selection rule for blind interpretation sign-off.

## Policy

1. Reviewer A and Reviewer B must be distinct people.
2. Each reviewer must be independent of the analysis team for the confirmatory run.
3. Each reviewer must declare COI disclosure as NONE before sign-off.
4. If the reviewers disagree, a distinct tie-break reviewer must adjudicate.
5. The tie-break reviewer must also be distinct from both original reviewers and independent.

## Enforcement

1. Reviewer names, independence declarations, and COI disclosures are recorded in `docs/generated/AI_BLIND_INTERPRETATION.md`.
2. The preregistration compliance gate blocks confirmatory interpretation if reviewer selection or disclosure requirements are not satisfied.
